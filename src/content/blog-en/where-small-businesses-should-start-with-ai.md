## Start with the most repetitive task

There are so many places where AI could be used that it is hard to know where to start. For a small business, the best starting point is the task that is **repeated most often and takes the most time**. In most businesses this falls into one of three areas: answering customer messages, managing appointments and bookings, or preparing reports from spreadsheets.

## What do the numbers say?

**Use is growing fast in Türkiye, but smaller businesses are lagging behind.** According to data from TÜİK, the Turkish Statistical Institute, the share of enterprises with 10 or more employees using AI was 2.7% in 2021. This rose to 7.5% in 2025 and 14% in 2026. However, the gap by size is very marked: the rate is 12.8% among enterprises with 10–49 employees, compared with 37.1% among those with 250 or more employees ([TÜİK, Yapay Zekâ İstatistikleri 2026 (in Turkish)](https://veriportali.tuik.gov.tr/tr/press/62066)).

**The most common purpose is marketing and sales.** According to the same data, more than half of enterprises using AI (51%) use it for marketing or sales.

**The biggest barrier for non-users is a lack of expertise.** The large majority (72.3%) of enterprises that have considered using AI but do not yet use it cite a lack of expertise as the barrier ([TÜİK bulletin (in Turkish)](https://veriportali.tuik.gov.tr/tr/press/62066); for commentary, see [Prof. Dr. Mahmut Özer, Milliyet (in Turkish)](https://www.milliyet.com.tr/yazarlar/prof-dr-mahmut-ozer/tuik-yapay-zeka-2026-yili-istatistikleri-ne-soyluyor-7672996)).

**Worldwide, companies are also trying AI quickly but are slow to scale it.** According to McKinsey's global survey, 88% of organisations were regularly using AI in at least one business function in 2025, up from 78% a year earlier. However, most organisations were still in the experimentation and pilot stage; only around a third had begun to scale their AI programmes across the company ([McKinsey, The State of AI](https://www.mckinsey.com/capabilities/operations/our-insights/the-state-of-ai)).

The lesson for small businesses is simple: trying AI out is not enough; it needs to be tied to real work.

## A 5-step plan to get started

**1. Keep notes on your work for a week.** Write down the tasks your team repeats every day and the time spent on them. List the questions customers ask most often separately.

**2. Choose a single process.** From the list, pick a task that is both frequently repeated and has clear rules. "Answering price and availability questions" is a good start; "making strategic decisions" is not.

**3. Measure before set-up.** Record the current situation for the process you have chosen: average response time to messages, number of missed messages, hours spent on reports. You will compare the results against this.

**4. Start with a small pilot.** Try it first on a single channel and a single process. Write down from the start what the assistant will answer and what it will hand over to your team.

**5. Measure, then expand.** After a few weeks, compare the results with your first measurement. If it is working, move on to a new channel or a new process.

## Which tasks suit AI?

| Suitable | Needs care |
|---|---|
| Answering frequently asked questions | Legal or medical advice |
| Booking appointments and sending reminders | Price negotiation and special discounts |
| Producing summary reports from spreadsheets | Complaint and crisis management |
| Classifying incoming requests | Processing personal and sensitive data |
| Drafting quotes and emails | Decisions that need final approval |

AI can help with the tasks in the "Needs care" column, but the final say should always rest with a person.

## 3 common mistakes

**Trying to automate everything at once.** As the scope grows, the set-up takes longer and the results become impossible to measure. Start with a single process.

**Starting without measuring.** If you do not record the situation before set-up, you cannot know whether AI is really delivering a benefit.

**Forgetting the route to a human.** An assistant cannot answer every question. How a conversation passes to your team when it cannot answer should be designed from the start.

## Conclusion

TÜİK data shows that the biggest barrier for businesses in Türkiye that have considered using AI but do not yet use it is a lack of expertise. You do not need to be an expert to start, though. You know your business best; choosing the right process, measuring it and starting small is enough.
