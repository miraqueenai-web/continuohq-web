## Business assistants are allowed; general-purpose bots are restricted

Meta has added a new section headed "AI providers" to the terms of use of the WhatsApp Business platform. This section prevents companies that develop AI models from distributing their own assistants through WhatsApp. The rule took effect on 15 October 2025 for new users and on 15 January 2026 for existing users ([TechCrunch](https://techcrunch.com/2025/10/18/whatssapp-changes-its-terms-to-bar-general-purpose-chatbots-from-its-platform)).

According to Meta's statement to TechCrunch, the change does not affect businesses that use AI to serve their customers on WhatsApp. The example Meta gave was a travel company using a bot for customer service. A Meta spokesperson said the purpose of the WhatsApp Business API is "to help businesses provide customer support and send relevant updates".

In other words, an assistant that answers a hotel's booking questions, books appointments for a beauty centre or sends reminders to a dietitian's clients is not the target of the rule.

## What exactly does the rule restrict?

At the heart of the restriction is the question of **whether the AI is the product itself**. Section 4.7 of the WhatsApp Business Solution Terms, headed "AI Providers", prohibits providers of technologies such as large language models, generative AI platforms and general-purpose AI assistants from using the WhatsApp Business Platform when those technologies are the primary functionality being made available (rather than incidental or ancillary functionality). The section also states that Meta decides at its own discretion what falls within this scope ([WhatsApp Business Solution Terms](https://www.whatsapp.com/legal/business-solution-terms)).

As a practical result, OpenAI and Microsoft announced that the ChatGPT and Copilot services on WhatsApp would end on 15 January 2026 ([Business Standard](https://www.business-standard.com/technology/tech-news/meta-bans-ai-chatbots-from-whatsapp-business-api-chatgpt-to-shut-down-jan-2026-125102200335_1.html), [TechRepublic](https://www.techrepublic.com/article/news-microsoft-copilot-leaving-whatsapp/)).

| Case | Targeted by the rule? |
|---|---|
| A hotel's assistant answering booking and availability questions | No |
| A beauty centre's assistant booking appointments and sending reminders | No |
| An e-commerce site's assistant reporting order status | No |
| A general, ChatGPT-style assistant that chats about any topic | Yes |

## 5 principles for setting up a compliant business assistant

**1. Define the assistant's scope by your business's services.** The assistant should answer questions directly related to your business, such as your services, pricing policy, opening hours and booking processes. For requests outside this scope, it should politely return to the topic or refer the customer to your team. An assistant with a clearly defined scope complies with the rules and also gives customers more consistent answers.

**2. Use the official infrastructure.** The assistant must run on the WhatsApp Business platform, using Meta's approved infrastructure. Running a personal WhatsApp account with automated software breaches the rules and can lead to the number being banned.

**3. Keep the route to a human open.** Conversations the assistant cannot resolve, and those that are sensitive or involve a complaint, should be passed to someone in your team. This matters for customer satisfaction and also helps prevent the assistant from giving incorrect information.

**4. Use conversation data only for your own assistant.** The same section does not allow data received through WhatsApp, including in anonymised form, to be used to develop or train any AI model. Fine-tuning is permitted only for a model used exclusively by the business. This means your conversations with customers can be used to improve your own assistant, and cannot be used for other purposes.

**5. Process personal data in line with KVKK, Türkiye's personal data protection law.** Inform your customers that they are messaging an assistant and how their data is processed. Where special categories of data such as health data are involved, additional safeguards are needed. You can find our own practice on our [Privacy Notice](/en/privacy-notice) page.

## What changes for your business?

The rule leaves businesses free to use AI on WhatsApp and only draws the limits. Meta wants WhatsApp to remain a channel where businesses talk to their customers.

The real question to ask is: "Is my assistant specific to my business?" An assistant trained on your business's information, working to your own rules and handing the conversation to your team when needed, is compliant and more useful for your customers.
