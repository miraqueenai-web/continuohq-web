## An assistant that takes on repetitive questions and keeps guests from waiting

A hotel reception answers the same questions over and over throughout the day: What time is check-in? Is there parking? Is breakfast included? Is there an airport transfer? These questions come by phone, WhatsApp, Instagram and the website, and often at the busiest moments at reception or in the middle of the night.

An AI receptionist answers these questions immediately, using your hotel's information and in the guest's own language. It takes guests who want to book to the booking step. When a complaint, a room change or a special request comes up, it hands the conversation to your reception team.

## What happens when a guest writes?

Let us take an example. At 02:14 a guest writes on WhatsApp: "Do you have a double room for 14–16 November?"

1. **It understands the question.** The assistant identifies the date range and the room type.
2. **It checks the information.** If it is connected to your booking system, it reads availability and the current price from there.
3. **It replies.** It sends the available room type, the price and the included services in the guest's language.
4. **It moves to the next step.** It sends the booking link or takes the guest's details.
5. **It hands over if needed.** If the guest makes a special request, it leaves a note for your team in the morning.

The guest gets an answer that night, instead of waiting for a competing hotel to reply in the morning.

## How much can the assistant take on?

The scope of an AI receptionist can be planned at two levels, depending on the systems your hotel uses.

**Response level.** The assistant is trained on your hotel's information; it answers questions, guides guests to the booking step and passes requests to your team. It can be put into service quickly, without connecting to any system.

**Integrated level.** The assistant connects to your property management system, channel manager or booking engine. In that case it checks availability and prices live, creates the booking directly, takes payment by sending a secure payment link, sends instant notifications to your team and reports on message traffic, conversion and incoming requests. This way it handles the booking itself as well as answering questions.

Which level suits you depends on the systems your hotel uses, the number of channels you receive messages on and your goals. The scope and cost of the set-up are shaped by this choice.

| Task | Response level | Integrated level |
|---|---|---|
| Availability, prices and room types | Answers from defined information | Live check |
| Booking | Guides to the booking step | Creates it directly |
| Payment | Guides to the payment page | Collects payment via a secure payment link |
| Room changes and special requests | Takes the request and passes it to the team | Checks availability and creates a request record |
| Complaints | First response, gathers details, passes to the team | The same, with a record in your ticketing system |
| Reporting | Conversation summaries | Message, conversion and revenue reports |

At both levels, some matters always stay under your team's control: if emergency phrases are defined, the assistant shares your phone number, and discounts outside the defined pricing policy and the resolution of sensitive complaints proceed with your team's approval. In these cases the assistant hands the conversation to your team together with the relevant details, so the guest does not have to explain everything again.

## 5 questions for the right set-up

Before setting up an AI receptionist, answer these questions:

1. **What are the 20 most frequent questions?** Look through the messages from the past few months and make a list. The assistant's knowledge base starts here.
2. **Which channels do messages come from?** WhatsApp, Instagram, the website, or all of them.
3. **Is your booking system open to integration?** This matters for keeping availability and prices up to date.
4. **Which matters must always go to the team?** Write the handover rules from the start.
5. **How will you measure success?** Before the set-up, record your average response time to messages and the share of messages that turn into bookings. After the set-up, compare using the same measures.

## Conclusion

An AI receptionist works alongside your team and takes repetitive questions and night-time messages off their hands. Once connected to your systems, it becomes part of the process from booking to payment. When it is set up properly, your guests do not wait and your team can spend its time looking after guests face to face.
